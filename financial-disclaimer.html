Financial Disclaimer

Important limitations and disclaimer for Finance Tools financial calculators and educational content.

Educational estimates only

Finance Tools provides calculators and explanations for general educational and planning purposes. Nothing on this website is financial, investment, tax, legal, accounting, lending or mortgage advice.

Mortgage and loan results

Actual payments and borrowing costs can include factors that a calculator may not model, including lender fees, points, taxes, insurance, PMI or other mortgage insurance, HOA dues, prepaid items, servicing rules, variable rates, credit-based pricing and product-specific terms.

Government-backed programs

FHA, VA and USDA calculators are educational models. Eligibility, fees, loan limits, funding or guarantee fees, insurance requirements and other program terms can depend on current official rules and individual circumstances.

Verify before acting

Before making a financial decision, verify current terms with the relevant lender, government agency, insurer, tax authority or qualified professional.
