How the Personal Loan Calculator Works

Enter the amount you want to borrow, the annual interest rate and the repayment term. The calculator uses a standard fixed-rate installment-loan formula to estimate the monthly principal-and-interest payment.

Personal Loan Payment Formula

For a fixed-rate loan, the payment depends on the loan principal, monthly interest rate and number of payments. When the interest rate is zero, the principal is divided evenly across the scheduled payments.

Example

For a $10,000 personal loan at 12% annual interest over five years, the calculator estimates a monthly principal-and-interest payment and shows how much interest would be paid over the scheduled term. Adding an extra monthly amount can reduce the modeled interest and payoff time.

What Can Affect Your Actual Personal Loan Cost?

  • Origination or administrative fees.
  • Loan term and interest rate offered by the lender.
  • Payment timing and rounding rules.
  • Late fees or other contractual charges.
  • Optional insurance or other products.
  • Prepayment terms, if applicable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a personal loan calculator?

It estimates monthly payments, total interest and total repayment for a fixed-rate installment loan.

How is a personal loan payment calculated?

The standard calculation uses the principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments.

Does it include loan fees?

No. The main calculation covers principal and interest; lender-specific fees are not included.

Can I make extra payments?

Many lenders allow extra principal payments, but you should check your loan agreement for applicable terms.

Does a lower interest rate reduce the payment?

For the same amount and term, a lower rate generally lowers the principal-and-interest payment and total interest.

Is the result exact?

No. It is an estimate based on the inputs and a standard fixed-rate loan model.
