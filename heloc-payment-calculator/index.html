How this HELOC calculator works

A home equity line of credit lets you borrow against available equity in your home. HELOCs commonly have a draw period followed by a repayment period, although terms vary by lender.

For an interest-only estimate, this calculator uses the current balance multiplied by the annual interest rate divided by 12. For a principal-and-interest estimate, it uses a standard fixed-rate payment formula over the repayment term.

HELOC draw period vs. repayment period

During a draw period, a borrower may be able to access available credit and may have an interest-only payment depending on the product. During repayment, principal generally must also be repaid. Read the specific HELOC agreement for the actual payment rules.

Example

With a $50,000 balance at an 8.5% annual rate, the simple interest-only estimate is about $354 per month. The actual payment can differ because lenders may use daily balances, variable rates, fees, or other contractual terms.

What can affect your actual HELOC cost?

Your rate, outstanding balance, lender fees, draw activity, repayment terms, minimum-payment rules and future rate changes can all affect the amount you pay.

HELOC payment FAQs

What is a HELOC?

A HELOC is a revolving line of credit secured by your home and generally based on available home equity.

How is a HELOC payment calculated?

Interest-only payments can be estimated from balance and rate. Principal-and-interest payments also depend on the repayment term.

Are HELOC rates fixed?

Many HELOCs have variable rates. This calculator holds the entered rate constant.

Does a HELOC use home equity?

Yes. The line is secured by the home and the available limit depends on lender rules and your financial circumstances.

Does this calculator include fees?

No. Lender and transaction fees are not included in these payment estimates.

Is this an approval calculator?

No. It estimates payments and does not determine eligibility or the amount a lender will approve.
