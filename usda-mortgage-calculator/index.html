HOW IT WORKS

How the USDA calculator works

The calculator subtracts the down payment from the home price to estimate the base loan. It then estimates the upfront guarantee fee and assumes it is financed into the mortgage. Principal and interest are calculated using a standard fixed-rate amortization formula.

The annual-fee estimate is simplified by applying the entered annual percentage to the starting loan balance and dividing by 12. USDA's actual annual fee methodology uses the average scheduled unpaid principal balance, so the real fee can decline as the loan amortizes.

FAQ

USDA mortgage calculator FAQs

Can USDA loans have zero down?

Yes, eligible Section 502 Guaranteed Loan borrowers can receive 100% financing when program and lender requirements are satisfied.

What is the upfront guarantee fee?

It is a USDA loan fee that may be financed. USDA's current FY2026 overview lists 1%.

What is the annual fee?

USDA's January 2026 overview lists 0.35%. The actual annual fee is calculated using USDA's prescribed methodology.

Do USDA loans require PMI?

USDA loans use a guarantee-fee structure rather than conventional private mortgage insurance. This calculator models the USDA upfront and annual fees instead.

Are USDA loans only for first-time buyers?

No. USDA materials state the program is not limited to first-time home buyers.

Does this calculator check eligibility?

No. Eligibility includes property location, household income, occupancy and other USDA and lender requirements.
