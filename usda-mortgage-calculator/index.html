HOW IT WORKS

How this USDA mortgage calculator works

The calculator starts with the purchase price minus your entered down payment to estimate the base loan. It then calculates the upfront USDA guarantee fee from the percentage you enter and assumes that fee is financed into the mortgage.

Principal and interest are calculated with a standard fixed-rate amortization formula. The monthly annual-fee estimate is simplified by applying the annual-fee percentage to the starting loan balance and dividing by 12.

USDA's actual annual-fee methodology is more detailed: USDA says the fee is based on the average scheduled unpaid principal balance, so the charge can change as the loan amortizes. This calculator therefore provides an estimate rather than a lender or USDA servicing calculation. Review USDA's annual-fee methodology.

USDA Rural Development's 2026 program materials list a 1% upfront guarantee fee and a 0.35% annual fee for Single Family Housing Guaranteed Loans. Fees are subject to program updates. Review the 2026 USDA program overview.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Example USDA mortgage calculation

Suppose you buy a $300,000 home with 0% down, a 6.25% interest rate and a 30-year term. The base loan is $300,000. With a 1% upfront guarantee fee, the estimated fee is $3,000, producing a starting financed loan of $303,000.

At 6.25% for 30 years, principal and interest on $303,000 is about $1,866 per month. The calculator's simplified 0.35% annual-fee estimate is about $88 per month. With 1.1% annual property tax ($275/month) and $1,800 annual homeowners insurance ($150/month), the estimated total is about $2,379 per month.

Your actual payment can differ because USDA's annual-fee calculation, interest rate, property taxes, insurance, eligible costs and lender terms may differ from these assumptions.

USDA ELIGIBILITY

USDA eligibility and rural-area requirements

This calculator estimates payment math; it does not determine whether a borrower or property qualifies for the USDA Section 502 Guaranteed Loan Program.

USDA says eligible applicants must meet income requirements, occupy the home as their primary residence and satisfy citizenship or eligible-status requirements. The property must be in an eligible rural area, and the loan is processed through an approved lender.

USDA states that the Guaranteed Loan Program can provide 100% financing for eligible borrowers, but no-down-payment financing does not mean every home or applicant qualifies. Household income, property eligibility, underwriting and lender requirements still apply.

FAQ

USDA mortgage calculator FAQs

Can a USDA loan have 0% down?

Yes. USDA Rural Development says eligible Section 502 Guaranteed Loan borrowers can receive 100% financing when program and lender requirements are met.

What is the USDA upfront guarantee fee?

It is a one-time guarantee fee associated with the USDA Guaranteed Loan Program. USDA's 2026 program materials list 1% for the Single Family Housing Guaranteed Loan Program, and the fee may be financed into the loan.

What is the USDA annual fee?

USDA's 2026 materials list an annual fee of 0.35%. The actual charge is based on USDA's prescribed annual-fee methodology rather than simply applying the percentage to the original loan balance for every month.

Does a USDA loan use PMI?

USDA Guaranteed Loans use an upfront guarantee fee and an annual fee rather than conventional private mortgage insurance. This calculator models those USDA-specific fees separately.

Are USDA loans only for first-time home buyers?

No. USDA materials state that the program is not limited to first-time buyers. Other eligibility requirements still apply.

Does this calculator determine USDA eligibility?

No. Eligibility can depend on household income, property location, occupancy, citizenship or eligible status, credit and lender underwriting. USDA and an approved lender make the applicable eligibility and underwriting determinations.

Does the calculator include taxes and insurance?

Yes. You can enter estimated annual property taxes and homeowners insurance, which the calculator adds to principal, interest and the estimated annual fee.

Does it include closing costs?

No. This calculator does not model every lender fee, prepaid item, appraisal charge or other transaction-specific cost. USDA rules may allow certain eligible purchase costs to be included in a transaction, but the treatment depends on the loan and transaction.

Educational estimate: This calculator is for planning only. USDA fees, eligibility rules, rural-area determinations, lender terms, taxes, insurance and other costs can change. Confirm current requirements with USDA Rural Development and an approved lender before relying on an estimate.
