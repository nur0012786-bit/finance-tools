HOW IT WORKS

How the down payment calculator works

The calculator multiplies the home price by your selected down payment percentage. It then subtracts the down payment from the purchase price to estimate the mortgage amount.

For planning purposes, it also compares the down payment and estimated closing costs with the cash you say is available for closing. Earnest money already paid is treated as a deposit that reduces the remaining amount needed.

The result is a budgeting estimate, not a lender approval or recommendation about how much you should put down.

US HOME-BUYING GUIDANCE

Down payment is only part of your upfront budget

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau recommends considering your available savings, other financial goals, moving costs, renovations and an emergency cushion before deciding how much cash to put toward a down payment. EXAMPLE

Example down payment comparison

On a $400,000 home, a 10% down payment is $40,000 and leaves an estimated $360,000 mortgage before other transaction adjustments. A 20% down payment is $80,000 and leaves an estimated $320,000 mortgage.

Compare those scenarios with your closing costs and cash reserves rather than looking at the down payment alone. A lower down payment may preserve more cash, while a larger one reduces the amount borrowed.

FAQ

Down payment calculator FAQs

Do I need 20% down?

No. Down payment requirements vary by loan program and lender. Some options allow substantially less than 20% down. citeturn0search3

Does a bigger down payment lower the loan amount?

Yes. For a given purchase price, every additional dollar paid upfront generally reduces the amount that must be financed.

Does a down payment include closing costs?

No. Closing costs are separate. Your total cash to close can include the down payment and closing costs, less deposits and credits that apply. citeturn0search11

Could a smaller down payment increase monthly costs?

It can. A smaller down payment means a larger loan, and mortgage insurance may also apply depending on the loan and circumstances. citeturn0search7

Should I use all my savings for the down payment?

Not necessarily. The CFPB recommends accounting for other goals, moving and renovation costs and an emergency cushion when determining how much cash is available for closing. citeturn0search1

Is this calculator a loan-qualification tool?

No. It estimates down payment and cash requirements from the numbers you enter. Lenders use their own underwriting criteria to determine eligibility and loan terms.

Educational estimate: This calculator does not determine loan eligibility or recommend a down payment. Loan programs, lender requirements, mortgage insurance, closing costs and cash-to-close figures can vary.
