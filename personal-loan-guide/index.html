PERSONAL LOANS

What is a personal loan?

Personal loans are commonly used for expenses such as debt consolidation, home projects, large purchases or other permitted purposes. The loan agreement determines the amount borrowed, interest rate, fees, repayment schedule and other terms.

What should you compare?

When comparing offers, look at the APR, interest rate, loan amount, repayment term, monthly payment, origination fee, late-payment terms and total amount scheduled to be repaid. A lower monthly payment can result from a longer term and does not by itself mean a lower total borrowing cost.

This is an educational example, not a quote or borrowing recommendation. Actual loan terms depend on the lender, applicant and applicable underwriting criteria.

Questions to ask before borrowing

  • What is the APR and interest rate?
  • Is there an origination fee or other upfront charge?
  • What is the exact repayment term and scheduled payment?
  • Can the loan be prepaid without a penalty?
  • What happens if a payment is late?
  • Which terms are conditional on autopay or other requirements?

Frequently asked questions

Are personal loan rates the same for everyone?

No. Lenders can consider factors such as credit history, income, existing obligations, loan amount, term and other underwriting criteria.

Is APR the same as the interest rate?

No. The interest rate measures the rate charged on the loan, while APR can incorporate certain finance charges and is intended to help compare borrowing costs. The exact calculation depends on the loan and applicable rules.

Can a calculator tell me whether I will qualify?

No. A calculator estimates payments from the assumptions you enter. A lender determines eligibility and actual terms.
