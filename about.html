About Finance Tools

Learn about Finance Tools, our calculator methodology, and our approach to clear US-focused financial education.

Simple tools for everyday financial questions

Finance Tools is a US-focused financial calculator website designed to help people explore common personal-finance scenarios with clear inputs, transparent estimates and practical explanations.

Our approach

Each calculator is designed around a specific financial question. We aim to show the assumptions used, explain what the result means, and identify important factors that the calculator does not model.

Educational, not personalized advice

Calculator results are estimates for planning and educational purposes. Real loan offers, rates, fees, taxes, insurance costs and account terms can differ by lender, location, product and individual circumstances.

Accuracy and feedback

We work to keep formulas, explanations and program-specific information accurate, but financial rules and lender practices can change. If you find an error or have a useful suggestion, please contact us.
