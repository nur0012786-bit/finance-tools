PROPERTY TAX GUIDE

How is property tax calculated?

A simple estimate starts with the taxable assessed value of a property and the applicable property tax rate. This calculator subtracts the annual exemption you enter from the assessed value, then applies the tax rate to the remaining taxable value.

Market value vs. assessed value

The home's market value and its assessed value are not necessarily the same. Local governments use their own assessment rules and schedules, so enter the assessed value from your local property records when you have it.

Why your mortgage payment may include property taxes

Property taxes are often collected through a mortgage escrow account. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explains that escrow lets a borrower pay property-related expenses through monthly amounts rather than one or two large bills, and that property taxes can change from year to year.

Property tax example

Suppose a property has an assessed value of $400,000, a 1.2% property tax rate and no exemption. The estimated annual tax is $4,800, or about $400 per month. Your actual bill can differ because local assessments, exemptions, special assessments and other jurisdiction-specific rules may apply.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What value should I use for property tax?

Use the assessed value used by your local taxing authority when it is available. If you only know the home's purchase or market value, treat the result as a rough estimate.

What property tax rate should I enter?

Enter the effective rate that applies to the property and jurisdiction. Rates and assessment practices vary across states, counties, cities and other taxing authorities.

Does this calculator include exemptions?

Yes. Enter an annual exemption amount and the calculator subtracts it from the assessed value before applying the tax rate. Eligibility and exemption amounts depend on local rules.

Does property tax affect my mortgage payment?

It can. When property taxes are included in escrow, the tax portion is collected with the mortgage payment. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau notes that escrow and total monthly payments can change when taxes or insurance change.

Are property taxes deductible on federal taxes?

State and local real property taxes can generally be deductible for taxpayers who itemize, subject to applicable federal rules and limits. This calculator does not estimate a tax deduction.

Is this property tax calculator tax advice?

No. It is an educational estimate. Check your local tax authority for the actual assessment, rate, exemptions and bill, and consult a qualified tax professional for tax questions.
