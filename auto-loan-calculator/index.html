AUTO LOAN GUIDE

How does an auto loan calculator work?

An auto loan calculator estimates the monthly payment from the amount financed, annual interest rate and repayment term. This version also lets you account for a down payment, trade-in value and an optional sales-tax estimate.

How does a down payment affect a car loan?

A down payment reduces the amount you need to finance. Borrowing less can reduce the monthly payment and the total interest paid, assuming the interest rate and term stay the same.

Why include trade-in value?

A trade-in can reduce the amount that needs to be financed, depending on the vehicle's value, any remaining loan balance and applicable local rules.

Auto loan example

Try a $30,000 vehicle with a $5,000 down payment, a 7% annual interest rate and a 5-year term. You can also add a trade-in value and sales tax to compare scenarios.

How this US auto loan calculator works

The calculator starts with the vehicle price, subtracts the down payment and trade-in value, and adds estimated sales tax to determine the financed amount. It then applies standard fixed-rate amortization to estimate the monthly principal-and-interest payment.

The model does not automatically include every dealer or government charge. Registration, title, documentation fees, warranties, maintenance, insurance and other add-ons can change the actual amount you pay.

Sales tax, trade-in and down payment

Sales tax can materially change the amount financed. A trade-in can reduce the amount you need to finance, while a larger down payment reduces principal and can reduce interest over the life of the loan.

Auto loan rate vs. APR

The interest rate measures the borrowing cost, while APR can include the interest rate plus certain fees. When comparing US auto financing offers, compare APRs with APRs and review the required disclosures.

Why loan term matters

A longer term can reduce the scheduled monthly payment but may increase total interest and keep you in debt longer. The CFPB also notes that a longer auto loan can increase the period during which you may have negative equity.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does an auto loan calculator calculate?

It estimates the amount financed, monthly payment, total interest and total scheduled payments using the values you enter.

Does a larger down payment lower the monthly car payment?

Generally, yes. A larger down payment means a smaller amount is financed, which can reduce the scheduled monthly payment and interest for the same rate and term.

Does this calculator include sales tax?

Yes. You can enter an estimated sales-tax percentage. The result is a simple estimate; actual tax treatment varies by location and transaction.

Does trade-in value reduce the loan amount?

The calculator subtracts the entered trade-in value from the vehicle price for an estimate. Actual financing can differ if there is a remaining balance on the trade-in or other transaction costs.

Does the calculator include insurance and registration?

No. Insurance, registration, title fees, dealer fees and other ownership costs are not included in the monthly payment estimate.
