UNDERSTAND THE NUMBER

What a mortgage payment really includes

A mortgage payment is often discussed as principal and interest, but a homeowner's monthly housing cost can be larger. This calculator separates principal and interest from estimated property taxes, homeowners insurance, PMI and HOA dues so you can test a more realistic budget.

Fannie Mae explains that a monthly housing expense can include principal, interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance and HOA or condo fees. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau distinguishes the principal-and-interest amount from a total payment that may also include taxes, insurance and mortgage insurance.

HOA dues are often paid separately, and taxes and insurance may be paid through escrow or directly by the homeowner. A monthly estimate should include them either way when planning total housing costs.

Why rate and term matter

30-year loan

Usually produces a lower scheduled principal-and-interest payment than a shorter term, but the borrower generally pays interest over more years.

15-year loan

Usually produces a higher scheduled payment but can reduce the repayment period and total interest when compared with the same loan amount and rate.

The CFPB explains that an APR is broader than the interest rate because it can include points, broker fees and other charges. When comparing real offers, review the Loan Estimate rather than treating the calculator's interest-rate input as an actual loan quote.

MORTGAGE RESEARCH

Use official documents for final decisions

A calculator is useful for scenario planning; it is not a substitute for a lender's disclosures. A Loan Estimate provides the actual quoted loan terms, estimated monthly payment and closing costs for a mortgage application. The CFPB recommends comparing Loan Estimates from different lenders.

For current rate research, use dated rate sources and record the date, loan type, term and assumptions. Do not hard-code a "today's rate" into a static calculator page unless you have a reliable data source and a process for updating it.

FAQ

Mortgage calculator questions

Does this calculator include property taxes?

Yes. Enter an estimated annual tax rate, and the calculator applies it to the entered home price and divides the annual estimate by 12. Actual assessments and tax bills depend on local rules.

Does it include homeowners insurance?

Yes. Enter an estimated annual homeowners insurance premium and the calculator divides it by 12. Actual premiums depend on the home, location, coverage, deductible and insurer.

How is PMI estimated?

The PMI input is treated as a flat annual percentage of the original loan amount and divided by 12. This is a simplified planning assumption only. Actual conventional PMI pricing and cancellation conditions depend on the borrower, loan, property and insurer. FHA and USDA fees follow different rules.

Does the total include HOA dues?

Yes. Enter the monthly HOA or condo dues. These charges are often paid separately from the mortgage servicer, but they still matter to your total monthly housing budget.

Does an extra payment change the scheduled monthly payment?

No. The primary monthly payment remains the required estimated housing payment. An extra principal payment is shown separately, and the calculator estimates how it changes payoff time and total interest if the same extra amount is paid each month.

How is cash to close estimated?

This simplified estimate adds the down payment and closing costs, then subtracts lender or seller credits applied to the estimated costs and earnest money already paid. Actual closing disclosures may include prepaid interest, tax and insurance escrows, prorations and other adjustments.

Does this calculator provide a mortgage offer?

No. It is an educational estimate. Actual rates, loan terms, insurance and fees depend on the lender, borrower, property and loan program.

Why might my lender's payment be different?

Your lender may use a different interest rate, loan amount, tax estimate, insurance premium, mortgage-insurance amount, escrow schedule or loan terms. Compare the complete payment and costs on your Loan Estimate.
