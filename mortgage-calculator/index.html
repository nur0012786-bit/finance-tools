UNDERSTAND THE NUMBER

What a mortgage payment really includes

A mortgage payment is often discussed as principal and interest, but a homeowner's monthly housing cost can be larger. This calculator separates principal and interest from estimated property taxes, homeowners insurance, PMI and HOA dues so you can test a more realistic budget.

Fannie Mae's mortgage calculator similarly explains that mortgage costs can include principal, interest, property taxes, mortgage insurance, homeowners insurance and HOA or condo fees. citeturn0search0

Why rate and term matter

30-year loan

Usually produces a lower scheduled principal-and-interest payment than a shorter term, but the borrower generally pays interest over more years.

15-year loan

Usually produces a higher scheduled payment but can reduce the repayment period and total interest when compared with the same loan amount and rate.

The CFPB explains that an APR is broader than the interest rate because it can include points, broker fees and other charges. When comparing real offers, review the Loan Estimate rather than treating the calculator's interest-rate input as an actual loan quote. citeturn0search4turn0search6

MORTGAGE RESEARCH

Use official documents for final decisions

A calculator is useful for scenario planning; it is not a substitute for a lender's disclosures. A Loan Estimate provides the actual quoted loan terms, estimated monthly payment and closing costs for a mortgage application. The CFPB recommends comparing Loan Estimates from different lenders. citeturn0search2turn0search13

For current rate research, use dated rate sources and record the date, loan type, term and assumptions. Do not hard-code a "today's rate" into a static calculator page unless you have a reliable data source and a process for updating it.

FAQ

Mortgage calculator questions

Does this calculator include property taxes?

Yes. Enter an estimated annual property-tax rate. The calculator converts it to a monthly estimate based on the home price.

Does it include homeowners insurance?

Yes. Enter your estimated annual premium and the calculator divides it by 12.

Does PMI automatically apply?

No. PMI is an assumption you control with the PMI field. Set it to 0 when it does not apply. Actual PMI pricing and cancellation rules depend on the loan and borrower.

Is the result a mortgage offer?

No. It is an educational estimate. Actual loan terms depend on the lender, borrower, property, loan program and other underwriting factors.

Should I use the calculator to decide how much I can afford?

Use it to test scenarios, not to establish a safe personal budget by itself. The CFPB distinguishes lender qualification from affordability and recommends considering the rest of your household finances. citeturn0search17
