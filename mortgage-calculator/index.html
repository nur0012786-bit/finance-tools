MORTGAGE GUIDE

How does a mortgage calculator work?

A mortgage calculator estimates the principal-and-interest portion of a fixed-rate home loan from the amount borrowed, annual interest rate and repayment term. The down payment reduces the amount financed from the home price.

How is the monthly mortgage payment calculated?

For a standard fixed-rate mortgage, the monthly principal-and-interest payment is calculated from the loan amount, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly payments. Each scheduled payment includes interest on the remaining balance and a portion that reduces principal.

For example, a $350,000 home with a $70,000 down payment leaves a $280,000 loan. At 6.5% interest over 30 years, the estimated principal-and-interest payment is about $1,770 per month. This example excludes taxes, insurance, PMI, HOA dues and closing costs.

What is included in this estimate?

This calculator estimates principal and interest only. It uses the home price, down payment, annual interest rate and loan term to calculate the financed amount and scheduled payment.

What is not included?

The estimate does not include property taxes, homeowners insurance, PMI, HOA dues, lender fees, prepaid costs or closing costs. These expenses can materially change the amount a homeowner actually pays each month or at closing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much should I enter for the down payment?

Enter the amount you expect to pay upfront toward the home's purchase price. The calculator subtracts it from the home price to estimate the loan amount.

Does this mortgage calculator include property taxes?

No. The estimate covers principal and interest only. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance and HOA fees are not included.

What happens if I change the interest rate?

A higher interest rate generally increases the estimated monthly principal-and-interest payment and total interest for the same loan amount and term.

What does a 30-year mortgage term mean?

A 30-year term means the scheduled loan repayment period is 30 years, or 360 monthly payments under a standard monthly amortization schedule.

Is this mortgage calculator financial advice?

No. It is an educational estimate. Actual loan terms and housing costs vary by lender, borrower, property and loan program.
