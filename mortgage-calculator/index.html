MORTGAGE GUIDE

How does a mortgage calculator work?

A mortgage calculator estimates the principal and interest portion of a home-loan payment from the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment term. The down payment reduces the amount borrowed from the lender.

What is included in this estimate?

This calculator estimates principal and interest only. A real monthly housing payment can also include property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance and other costs depending on the loan and property.

Mortgage example

Try a $350,000 home with a $70,000 down payment, a 6.5% annual interest rate and a 30-year term to see an estimated principal-and-interest payment.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much should I enter for the down payment?

Enter the amount you expect to pay upfront toward the home's purchase price. The calculator subtracts it from the home price to estimate the loan amount.

Does this mortgage calculator include property taxes?

No. The estimate covers principal and interest only. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance and HOA fees are not included.

What happens if I change the interest rate?

A higher interest rate generally increases the estimated monthly principal-and-interest payment and total interest for the same loan amount and term.

What does a 30-year mortgage term mean?

A 30-year term means the scheduled loan repayment period is 30 years, or 360 monthly payments under a standard monthly amortization schedule.

Is this mortgage calculator financial advice?

No. It is an educational estimate. Actual loan terms and housing costs vary by lender, borrower, property and loan program.
