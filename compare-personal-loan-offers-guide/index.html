How to Compare Personal Loan Offers

Learn a practical framework for comparing personal loan offers using APR, payment, term, fees, total repayment and loan features.

Educational finance guidance

Comparing personal loans is easier when the offers are evaluated using the same loan amount and time horizon. Look beyond a single advertised rate.

Start with the same borrowing amount

For a fair comparison, compare offers for similar loan amounts. Differences in amount can change both payment and total cost.

Compare APR and rate

Review both the interest rate and APR. APR can incorporate certain finance charges and can help compare similar loans with different fees.

Compare term and payment

A longer term may lower the required payment but can increase total interest. Put the monthly payment and total repayment side by side.

Check fees and conditions

Look for origination fees, late fees, autopay conditions, prepayment provisions and any other disclosed costs.

Illustrative comparison

Imagine two $15,000 offers. Offer A has a lower rate but an upfront fee, while Offer B has a slightly higher rate and fewer fees. Comparing APR, payment and total repayment provides more context than comparing the advertised rate alone.

Frequently asked questions

Will a calculator tell me what I should borrow or spend?

No. Calculators are educational tools that apply the assumptions entered by the user. Actual borrowing terms, eligibility and personal affordability require additional information.

Are loan and credit rules the same for every lender?

No. Lenders can have different product terms, underwriting criteria, fees and disclosures. The lender's agreement and disclosures control an actual transaction.

Where should I verify the terms of an actual account?

Review the lender or card issuer's official disclosures, statements and agreement, and ask the provider about any item you do not understand.
