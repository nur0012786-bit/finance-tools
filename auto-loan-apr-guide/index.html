Educational finance guidance

Auto loan APR provides an annualized measure intended to help describe the cost of credit. It should be reviewed together with the rate, financed amount, term and fees.

Interest rate vs. APR

The interest rate is the rate used to calculate interest under the loan terms. APR can include certain finance charges and can help compare similar credit offers.

Why APR matters on a car loan

Two auto loans can have similar rates but different fees or other costs. APR can provide an additional comparison point.

APR is not the same as payment

The payment depends on the amount financed, rate, term and payment structure. APR alone cannot determine the monthly payment.

What else to review

Check the vehicle price, amount financed, down payment, trade-in, term, rate, APR, fees and total scheduled repayment.

Illustrative example

A lender offer with a slightly lower rate but higher applicable finance charges may have an APR that provides a different comparison from the rate alone.

Frequently asked questions

Will a calculator tell me what I should borrow or spend?

No. Calculators are educational tools that apply the assumptions entered by the user. Actual borrowing terms, eligibility and personal affordability require additional information.

Are loan and credit rules the same for every lender?

No. Lenders can have different product terms, underwriting criteria, fees and disclosures. The lender's agreement and disclosures control an actual transaction.

Where should I verify the terms of an actual account?

Review the lender or card issuer's official disclosures, statements and agreement, and ask the provider about any item you do not understand.
