FHA LOAN CALCULATION

How this FHA mortgage calculator works

This calculator models a standard FHA purchase mortgage. It estimates the base loan after the down payment, the upfront mortgage insurance premium (UFMIP), principal and interest, the first-year monthly mortgage insurance premium (MIP), property taxes and homeowners insurance. It is a planning estimate, not an FHA approval decision or a lender quote.

1. Base loan and upfront mortgage insurance

The base loan is the purchase price minus the down payment. For most standard FHA forward mortgages, HUD lists UFMIP at 1.75% of the base loan amount. If you choose to finance UFMIP, it is added to the mortgage balance used to calculate principal and interest. If you choose to pay it in cash, the fee is shown separately and is not added to the mortgage balance.

Example: a $337,750 base loan has estimated UFMIP of $5,910.63 at 1.75%. If financed, the starting mortgage balance before whole-dollar closing adjustments is about $343,660.63.

2. Annual MIP rate is selected from HUD's schedule

The annual MIP rate is not one universal percentage. HUD's published schedule uses the mortgage term, base loan amount and loan-to-value (LTV) ratio. This calculator selects the standard forward-mortgage rate from those inputs instead of asking you to guess the annual MIP rate.

The current HUD schedule distinguishes base loan amounts of $726,200 or less from amounts above $726,200. That $726,200 figure is the threshold in HUD's published MIP rate table; it is not the same thing as the local FHA mortgage limit. The MIP schedule also changes for terms of 15 years or less.

3. First-year monthly MIP estimate

HUD's monthly MIP calculation approach uses the average scheduled outstanding balance over the amortization year, rather than simply multiplying the original starting balance by the annual rate. This estimator calculates the average of the scheduled beginning balances for the first 12 monthly periods, applies the HUD annual MIP rate, adjusts for financed UFMIP using the published premium factor, and divides the annual amount by 12.

This is an independent planning estimate. FHA-approved lenders use the applicable case information, HUD systems, required rounding and servicing calculations to establish the premium billed for a specific loan. Later annual MIP amounts can change as the mortgage balance declines.

4. Taxes and homeowners insurance

Property tax is estimated by applying the annual percentage you enter to the purchase price and dividing by 12. Actual tax bills depend on local assessments, exemptions and tax rates. Homeowners insurance is the annual premium you enter divided by 12. The calculator does not include HOA dues, utilities, maintenance or every closing cost.

2026 FHA MORTGAGE LIMITS

FHA loan limits depend on the property location

For calendar year 2026, HUD lists a one-unit national FHA forward-mortgage floor of $541,287 and a high-cost ceiling of $1,249,125. The actual limit is set by county or metropolitan area and property size, and special exception areas can have different limits. Do not treat the national floor or ceiling as the exact limit for every property.

The MIP rate table uses a separate base-loan threshold of $726,200. That threshold is used to select an annual MIP rate; it is not the county loan limit and does not replace checking the applicable local FHA mortgage limit.

HOW LONG MIP LASTS

When does FHA monthly MIP end?

For FHA case numbers assigned on or after June 3, 2013, HUD says annual MIP is collected for the maximum duration in the Handbook: when original LTV is greater than 90%, MIP generally continues until the end of the loan term or 30 years, whichever occurs first; when original LTV is 90% or less, it generally continues until the end of the term or 11 years, whichever occurs first.

Borrowers cannot request early cancellation of annual MIP solely because they paid down principal. Paying extra can reduce interest and the loan balance, but it does not by itself change the FHA MIP duration rule. This calculator displays the duration indicated by the published rate table for the inputs entered.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FHA mortgage calculator FAQs

How much is FHA upfront mortgage insurance?

For most standard FHA forward mortgages, UFMIP is 1.75% of the base loan amount. HUD lists exceptions for certain refinance and special-property programs. This calculator models the standard purchase case.

How is the annual FHA MIP rate selected?

For standard forward mortgages, HUD's table uses the base loan amount, LTV ratio and mortgage term. This calculator selects the standard rate from those inputs and shows its estimated duration.

Why is annual MIP not just the original loan multiplied by the rate?

HUD's monthly premium calculation approach uses the average scheduled outstanding balance over the amortization year. This calculator estimates the first-year monthly premium from that average balance and adjusts for financed UFMIP. A lender's FHA system calculation and required rounding determine the actual billed amount.

Does financing UFMIP increase my monthly payment?

Yes. If UFMIP is financed, it increases the mortgage balance on which principal and interest are calculated. Paying it in cash keeps it out of the principal balance but increases cash needed at closing.

Does FHA MIP last for the entire loan?

It depends on the original LTV and applicable FHA rules. For many standard case numbers assigned on or after June 3, 2013, MIP lasts 11 years when original LTV is 90% or less and for the loan term (up to 30 years) when original LTV is above 90%.

Can I cancel FHA MIP by paying extra principal?

For case numbers assigned on or after June 3, 2013, HUD says borrowers may not request early cancellation of annual MIP based on principal prepayment. Review your case-specific rules with your servicer.

Are FHA loan limits the same in every county?

No. FHA forward mortgage limits depend on the county or metropolitan area and the number of units. Use HUD's official mortgage-limit lookup for the property address.

Does this calculator determine FHA eligibility or approval?

No. It estimates payment components. FHA eligibility, credit approval, debt-to-income review, property acceptability and underwriting are determined by the lender under HUD rules.
