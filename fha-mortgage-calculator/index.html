HOW IT WORKS

How the FHA calculator works

The calculator subtracts your down payment from the home price to estimate the base mortgage. It then estimates upfront MIP and, if financed, adds that amount to the starting loan balance. Principal and interest are calculated using a standard fixed-rate amortization formula.

The monthly MIP estimate uses the annual MIP percentage entered above divided by 12 and applied to the starting loan balance. Actual FHA MIP billing is more detailed and can use annual average outstanding balance calculations, so this tool should be treated as an estimate.

FAQ

FHA mortgage calculator FAQs

What is an FHA mortgage calculator?

It estimates key costs for an FHA-style mortgage, including the base loan, upfront MIP, monthly MIP and principal-and-interest payment.

How much down payment is required?

FHA 203(b) can provide approximately 96.5% financing for eligible borrowers, corresponding to about 3.5% down. Lender and borrower requirements still apply.

What is UFMIP?

UFMIP is the upfront mortgage insurance premium. HUD says it is required for most FHA single-family mortgage insurance programs and may be financed.

Do FHA loan limits vary?

Yes. HUD publishes limits by county or metropolitan area and property size. The national floor and ceiling do not represent the exact limit for every location.

Does this calculate FHA approval?

No. This tool does not determine eligibility or approval. A lender evaluates income, debts, credit, property, documentation and other underwriting factors.

Does it include closing costs?

No. Closing costs, lender fees, discount points and other transaction-specific charges are not included in the estimate.
