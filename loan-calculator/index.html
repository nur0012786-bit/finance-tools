HOW IT WORKS

Loan payment formula

For a standard fixed-rate amortizing loan, the monthly payment is calculated from the principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. The calculator converts the annual rate to a monthly rate and applies the standard amortization formula.

Why extra payments can save interest

An extra payment reduces the outstanding principal sooner. Because future interest is calculated on the remaining balance, reducing principal earlier can reduce total interest and shorten the payoff period.

Example

Try a $25,000 loan at 7% over 5 years and enter $100 as the extra monthly payment to see the estimated time and interest savings.
