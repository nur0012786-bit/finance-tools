HOW IT WORKS

How this VA mortgage calculator works

The calculator subtracts the down payment from the purchase price to estimate the base loan. It then applies a standard VA purchase-loan funding-fee rate according to whether you select first or subsequent use and the percentage down.

Under the standard rate schedule for VA purchase or construction loans:

  • First use: 2.15% with less than 5% down; 1.5% with 5% or more down; 1.25% with 10% or more down.
  • Subsequent use: 3.3% with less than 5% down; 1.5% with 5% or more down; 1.25% with 10% or more down.

The current published table applies to loans closing from April 7, 2023 through June 8, 2034. The calculator uses the standard schedule; it does not handle every borrower category or every VA loan type. Your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) and lender disclosures are the final references for your fee. View the VA funding-fee schedule and later VA circular updates.

Some borrowers are exempt from the funding fee, including certain Veterans receiving or eligible for VA disability compensation and certain surviving spouses receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation. Other circumstances can also qualify. The calculator cannot determine your exemption; confirm it with VA or your lender before selecting the exemption option. Read VA funding-fee and exemption guidance.

If you finance the fee, it is added to the base loan and the monthly principal-and-interest payment is calculated on the larger starting balance. If you pay it at closing, it is not added to the loan balance. The cash-needed result includes your down payment and any fee paid upfront, but excludes other closing costs, prepaid items and escrow deposits.

The monthly estimate adds property taxes, homeowners insurance and the HOA dues you enter. The property-tax calculation uses your entered annual percentage times the home price; actual tax bills are based on local assessment rules and may differ. Utilities, maintenance and repairs are not included.

VA-backed purchase loans do not require monthly private mortgage insurance (PMI). This page estimates a VA-backed purchase loan, not an IRRRL, cash-out refinance, assumption, or other VA loan type. VA says only the funding fee may be financed into a VA purchase loan; other closing costs must be paid at closing, subject to applicable seller credits. See VA loan fees and closing costs.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Example VA mortgage calculation

Assume a $400,000 purchase, no down payment, first use, no funding-fee exemption, a 30-year term and a 6.25% annual rate. Under the standard first-use purchase schedule for less than 5% down, the funding-fee rate is 2.15%. The base loan is $400,000; the estimated fee is $8,600; and financing the fee produces a starting loan of $408,600.

The estimated monthly principal and interest on $408,600 is $2,515.82. With property tax estimated at 1.1% of the home price per year ($366.67 per month), homeowners insurance of $1,800 per year ($150 per month), and no HOA dues, the estimated total is $3,032.49 per month. This is an illustration, not a lender quote or an eligibility decision.

VA LOAN CONTEXT

VA entitlement, loan limits and eligibility

A payment calculator cannot determine whether you qualify for a VA-backed loan. Eligibility generally requires a valid Certificate of Eligibility (COE), compliance with VA and lender requirements, and a home that meets applicable property and occupancy rules.

Eligible borrowers may be able to buy with no down payment when the purchase price does not exceed the home's appraised value. Entitlement and guaranty rules are more specific than a single nationwide loan-limit figure. Your COE and lender help establish available entitlement and the amount VA can guaranty.

This calculator does not assess entitlement, residual income, debt-to-income limits, appraisal value, property eligibility or underwriting. Do not treat its loan amount as a VA approval. Confirm your eligibility and loan structure with VA and a VA-approved lender. Review VA purchase-loan eligibility.

FAQ

VA mortgage calculator FAQs

Does a VA loan require PMI?

No. VA states that its home loan program does not require monthly private mortgage insurance.

Can I use a VA loan with no down payment?

Eligible borrowers may be able to purchase with no down payment when their entitlement, the property, the loan and lender requirements support the transaction.

What is the VA funding fee?

It is a one-time charge on many VA-backed or VA direct home loans. The rate depends on factors such as loan type, down payment and whether the benefit is being used for the first or a subsequent time. Certain borrowers are exempt.

Can I finance the VA funding fee?

For a VA purchase loan, the funding fee may generally be financed into the loan or paid at closing. Financing it increases the starting principal and the interest calculated over time. Other purchase closing costs generally cannot be added to the VA loan amount.

How does the calculator choose the funding-fee rate?

It applies the standard purchase-loan schedule using first or subsequent use and your down-payment tier. The COE and lender disclosures should be used to confirm your actual funding fee.

Does the calculator determine my VA eligibility?

No. It is a payment calculator, not an eligibility or underwriting tool. A lender and VA eligibility documentation such as a COE are needed to establish eligibility.

Does the calculator include closing costs?

No. The cash-needed result includes only the down payment and, when selected, the upfront funding fee. Lender fees, appraisal charges, prepaid items, discount points, escrow deposits and other transaction costs are excluded.

Does it include property taxes and homeowners insurance?

Yes, when you enter estimates for those costs. They are added to principal and interest to produce the total estimated monthly payment.
