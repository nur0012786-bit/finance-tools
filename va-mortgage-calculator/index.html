HOW IT WORKS

How the VA calculator works

The calculator subtracts your down payment from the purchase price to estimate the base loan. If you choose to finance the VA funding fee, the fee is added to the loan before principal and interest are calculated.

FAQ

VA mortgage calculator FAQs

Does a VA loan require PMI?

No. VA states that its home loan program does not require monthly private mortgage insurance.

Can a VA loan have no down payment?

Eligible borrowers may qualify for no-down-payment financing when VA and lender requirements are met.

What is the funding fee?

It is a one-time fee on many VA-backed or VA direct loans. The amount depends on circumstances such as down payment and prior use, and some borrowers are exempt.

Can the funding fee be financed?

Yes. VA says the funding fee may be included in the loan. Other purchase-loan closing costs generally cannot simply be financed into the loan.

Does this calculator check my VA eligibility?

No. A lender will verify eligibility, including the Certificate of Eligibility and underwriting requirements.

Does it include closing costs?

No. Lender fees, appraisal and other transaction-specific closing costs are excluded from this estimate.
