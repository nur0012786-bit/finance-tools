HOW IT WORKS

How this VA mortgage calculator works

The calculator first subtracts the down payment from the purchase price to estimate the base VA loan. It then estimates the one-time funding fee from the percentage you enter.

If you choose to finance the fee, the fee is added to the base loan and the resulting balance is used to calculate principal and interest. If you choose to pay the fee in cash, the monthly principal-and-interest calculation uses only the base loan. VA says the funding fee can generally be paid at closing or financed into the loan. Some eligible borrowers are exempt. See VA funding-fee rules.

The monthly estimate then adds property tax and homeowners insurance. The calculator does not add a monthly private mortgage insurance charge because VA states that its home loan program does not require monthly private mortgage insurance.

For VA-backed purchase loans, the current VA funding-fee schedule lists 2.15% for first use with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% or more down, and 1.25% with 10% or more down. For subsequent use, the rates are 3.3% with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% or more, and 1.25% with 10% or more. These percentages are inputs so you can model the rate that applies to your situation. WORKED EXAMPLE

Example VA mortgage calculation

Suppose you buy a $400,000 home with no down payment, a 6.25% interest rate and a 30-year term. Using the default 2.15% funding fee, the base loan is $400,000 and the estimated funding fee is $8,600. If the fee is financed, the starting loan becomes $408,600 before principal and interest are calculated.

At 6.25% over 30 years, the estimated monthly principal and interest on $408,600 is about $2,515. Adding 1.1% annual property tax ($367/month) and $1,800 annual homeowners insurance ($150/month) produces an estimated total monthly payment of about $3,032. Your actual payment can differ because your rate, taxes, insurance, funding-fee status and lender terms may be different.

VA LOAN CONTEXT

VA entitlement, loan limits and eligibility

A VA mortgage calculator can estimate payment math, but it cannot determine whether you qualify for a VA-backed loan. Eligibility and underwriting involve VA benefit eligibility, a Certificate of Eligibility (COE), income, credit, debts, the property and lender requirements.

VA's entitlement rules are also more specific than a simple nationwide loan-limit number. Your COE shows entitlement, and remaining bonus entitlement can be calculated using the one-unit county loan limit. VA explains that lenders may require a down payment when available entitlement is not enough to support the requested loan amount under their guaranty requirements. If you have sufficient entitlement, a VA-backed loan may be available above the county loan limit, subject to lender approval and other requirements.

For that reason, this calculator does not label a loan amount as automatically VA-eligible. Use it for payment planning and confirm your entitlement and loan structure with a VA-approved lender.

FAQ

VA mortgage calculator FAQs

Does a VA loan require PMI?

No. VA states that its home loan program does not require monthly private mortgage insurance.

Can I use a VA loan with no down payment?

Eligible borrowers may be able to purchase with no down payment when their entitlement, the property, the loan and lender requirements support the transaction.

What is the VA funding fee?

It is a one-time charge on many VA-backed or VA direct home loans. The rate depends on factors such as loan type, down payment and whether the benefit is being used for the first or a subsequent time. Certain borrowers are exempt.

Can I finance the VA funding fee?

Yes. VA says the funding fee can generally be included in the loan or paid in full at closing. Financing it increases the balance on which interest is calculated.

Why is the funding fee set to 2.15%?

The default models a first-use purchase with less than 5% down. VA's current purchase-loan table uses different rates for 5% and 10% down payments and for subsequent use, so you should change the input when your situation differs.

Does the calculator determine my VA eligibility?

No. It is a payment calculator, not an eligibility or underwriting tool. A lender and VA eligibility documentation such as a COE are needed to establish eligibility.

Does the calculator include closing costs?

No. Lender fees, appraisal charges, prepaid items, discount points and other transaction-specific closing costs are not included in the monthly estimate.

Does it include property taxes and homeowners insurance?

Yes, when you enter estimates for those costs. They are added to principal and interest to produce the total estimated monthly payment.

Educational estimate: This calculator is for planning only. VA funding-fee rules, entitlement, lender requirements, interest rates, property taxes, insurance and other costs can vary. Confirm current requirements with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs and your lender before relying on an estimate.
